Logical Model: Trade Item

Official URL: http://smart.who.int/pcmt/StructureDefinition/TradeItem Version: 0.2.0
Draft as of 2025-07-28 Computable Name: TradeItem

A Product represents a physical item or set of items which can be held in inventory, ordered, exchanged, dispensed or other utilized within the health system, clinical and supply chain workflows.

Usages:

  • Refer to this Logical Model: Regulated Trade Item and Trade Item
